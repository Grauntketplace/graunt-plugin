# Wanted: Medical device recalls joined to GUDID device identifiers, weekly

_Category: `health-safety`_

**What**
recall number, classification, date, firm, product description, reason, affected UDI-DIs and lot/serial ranges where stated, GUDID brand/model/company fields, source URLs, snapshot date.

**Task it serves**
"Do any devices in this inventory export match an open Class I recall?"

**Coverage and freshness**
US; weekly.

**Rights a buyer needs**
FDA data, public domain. Redistribution needed.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
FDA recall database and AccessGUDID downloads; the join is the work.

**Why this is on the board**
Hospital supply-chain agents cannot join these two sources reliably today.
