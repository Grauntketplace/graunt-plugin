# Wanted: Job title ↔ SOC 2018 ↔ O*NET-SOC crosswalk with skills and typical education, annual

_Category: `industry-reference`_

**What**
raw title (from postings and O*NET alternate titles), SOC 2018 code and title, O*NET-SOC code, match confidence, top skills/knowledge/abilities with importance scores, typical education, BLS median wage reference, O*NET release, snapshot date.

**Task it serves**
"Classify these 50,000 job postings to SOC codes and list the top skills per code."

**Coverage and freshness**
All SOC codes; annual with O*NET releases, alternate-title list refreshed quarterly.

**Rights a buyer needs**
O*NET is CC-BY-4.0, attribution propagates; BLS public domain. Redistribution needed.

**Format**
CSV plus Parquet.

**Where a seller could start**
O*NET database and alternate titles, BLS OES.

**Why this is on the board**
HR and labour-market agents need the messy-title layer, which O*NET alone does not give.
