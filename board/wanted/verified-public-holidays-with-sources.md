# Wanted: Public holidays 2026–2028 for 60 countries with a government source URL per entry (verified tier)

_Category: `geo-reference`_

**What**
country, subdivision, date, holiday name (local and English), type (public/bank/regional), observed rule applied, declaring authority, source URL, date verified.

**Task it serves**
"Build a business-day calendar for our Singapore, Poland and Brazil offices for 2027 that I can defend to auditors."

**Coverage and freshness**
The 60 largest economies; annual, published each October for the following year, with corrections as gazettes update.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Original compilation of public facts. Redistribution and training allowed; citation appreciated.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
Official gazettes and ministry announcements. The free seed packets on this repository's releases show the target schema; the value here is the per-entry source.

**Why this is on the board**
Payroll and scheduling agents need a source they can cite, not a library's best guess.
