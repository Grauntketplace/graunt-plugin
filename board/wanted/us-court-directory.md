# Wanted: US federal and state court directory with e-filing URLs, local-rules links and clerk contacts

_Category: `legal-regulatory`_

**What**
court id, name, level, jurisdiction (state/county/district), address, clerk phone and general email, website, e-filing system and URL, local rules URL, docket search URL, time zone, last verified date.

**Task it serves**
"Where do I e-file a civil complaint in Travis County, and what are the local rules?"

**Coverage and freshness**
All federal courts and all state trial and appellate courts (about 3,500 records); quarterly verification.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Internal use and redistribution. Official contacts in official capacity; declare as personal data where a named clerk appears.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
Court websites, state AOC directories, PACER court locator. Compile and verify.

**Why this is on the board**
Legal-ops agents hallucinate court URLs; a verified directory with dates fixes that.
