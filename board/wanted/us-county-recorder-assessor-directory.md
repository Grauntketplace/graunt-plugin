# Wanted: US county recorder and assessor office directory with online-search URLs and fee schedules

_Category: `geo-reference`_

**What**
county FIPS and name, recorder office name, address, phone, online records search URL and vendor, assessor parcel search URL, GIS portal URL, copy and recording fee schedule reference, e-recording availability, verified date.

**Task it serves**
"Pull the deed history for this parcel in Maricopa County" and "which counties in Georgia support e-recording?"

**Coverage and freshness**
All 3,143 counties and equivalents; annual, with a change log.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Compiled from public office pages. Redistribution needed.

**Format**
CSV.

**Where a seller could start**
County websites, state associations of counties, e-recording vendor coverage lists.

**Why this is on the board**
Real-estate and title agents lose hours to broken links.
