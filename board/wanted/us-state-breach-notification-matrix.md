# Wanted: US state data-breach notification requirements matrix with statute citations, quarterly

_Category: `legal-regulatory`_

**What**
state, statute citation and URL, definition of personal information (structured elements), harm threshold, individual-notice deadline, regulator-notice trigger and deadline, consumer-reporting-agency notice trigger, required notice content, encryption safe harbour, penalties, last amended, verified date.

**Task it serves**
"We lost 1,200 records of Texas and Illinois residents including driver's licence numbers; what must we do and by when, with citations?"

**Coverage and freshness**
50 states + DC + territories; quarterly.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Original compilation from public statutes. Redistribution to the buyer's clients needed.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
State statutes (official sites). This is analysis work; declare it as the seller's own compilation with citations.

**Why this is on the board**
Incident-response agents need a matrix with citations, not a law-firm PDF.
