# Wanted: US Harmonized Tariff Schedule with duty rates, special-program indicators and effective dates, per revision

_Category: `finance-tax`_

**What**
HTS 10-digit code, description hierarchy, unit of quantity, general rate, special rates and program codes, column 2 rate, additional duties (e.g. Section 301/232 flags), revision number and effective date, source URL.

**Task it serves**
"Classify and rate this SKU for import from Vietnam as of the current revision, with citation."

**Coverage and freshness**
Full schedule; per USITC revision (several per year).

**Rights a buyer needs**
US government work, public domain. Redistribution and training allowed.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
USITC HTS downloads (JSON/CSV) per revision.

**Why this is on the board**
Customs and trade-compliance agents need it structured with the revision pinned.
