# Wanted: Exchange trading calendars 2025–2027 (NYSE, Nasdaq, CME, LSE, Euronext, Xetra, TSE, HKEX, SGX, ASX) with early closes and sources

_Category: `finance-tax`_

**What**
exchange (MIC), date, status (closed / early close), close time, settlement convention (T+1/T+2), source notice URL, last verified date.

**Task it serves**
"Compute the settlement date for a Euronext Paris trade on 2026-12-23" and "is CME Globex open on Juneteenth 2027?"

**Coverage and freshness**
Ten venues listed, 2025 through 2027; annual refresh plus ad-hoc when an exchange amends its calendar.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Internal use and redistribution in downstream products. Attribution fine.

**Format**
CSV, one row per exchange-date.

**Where a seller could start**
Each exchange publishes its calendar and notices. Compile, cite, and record the verification date.

**Why this is on the board**
Treasury, trading and back-office agents get settlement dates wrong on half days; the packages that exist rarely cite the notice.
