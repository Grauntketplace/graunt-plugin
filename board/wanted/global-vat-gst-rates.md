# Wanted: Global VAT and GST rates with reduced-rate categories, registration thresholds, effective dates and sources

_Category: `finance-tax`_

**What**
country/territory, rate type (standard/reduced/super-reduced/zero/exempt), rate, category description (goods/services it applies to), registration threshold and currency, effective date, source URL, last verified date.

**Task it serves**
"What VAT rate applies to an e-book sold to a consumer in Portugal today, and since when?"

**Coverage and freshness**
All countries with VAT/GST (about 175); monthly verification, changes flagged.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Internal use and redistribution in invoicing. Attribution fine.

**Format**
CSV plus a change log CSV.

**Where a seller could start**
National tax authorities and official gazettes. The compilation and the reduced-rate categories are the work.

**Why this is on the board**
Cross-border commerce agents need reduced-rate categories, which free tables omit.
