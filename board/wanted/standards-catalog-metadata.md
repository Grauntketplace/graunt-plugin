# Wanted: Standards catalogue metadata (ISO, IEC, IEEE, ASTM, NIST SP): identifier, title, status, edition, dates, supersession

_Category: `industry-reference`_

**What**
publisher, identifier, edition/year, title, status (current/withdrawn/draft), publication date, withdrawal date, supersedes / superseded-by, ICS codes, canonical URL, snapshot date.

**Task it serves**
"Is ISO 27001:2013 still current, what replaced it, and what is the exact title to cite?"

**Coverage and freshness**
Publishers named; quarterly.

**Rights a buyer needs**
Bibliographic metadata compiled from public catalogues; declare each publisher's catalogue terms. Redistribution needed.

**Format**
CSV plus JSON.

**Where a seller could start**
Publisher catalogues and OBP. Text of standards is out of scope and must not be included.

**Why this is on the board**
Engineering and compliance agents cite withdrawn editions constantly.
